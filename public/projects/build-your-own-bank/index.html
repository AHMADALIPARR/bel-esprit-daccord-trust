Banking product development

Build Your Own Bank

ProblemApproachScopeLicense

Trust intellectual property

Bel Esprit d’Accord Trust intellectual-property portfolio. Owner: Ahmad Ali Parr.

Problem

Give developers a source foundation for building their own banking workflows and integrations.

Approach

A product assembled from the existing DevFlow Finance Twin banking code: C# payment rails, COBOL/RPG/PL-I treasury and gateway components, Db2 schemas, native WORM storage, and ledger runtimes.

Verification and limits

Development is active. Banking integration requires the original host contracts, IBM environment and deployment-specific configuration. Repository access is restricted; contact the trust for access and licensing.

This project summary reviews public documentation. It does not substitute for the project's build, tests, proof audit, or independent evaluation.
