Conservation-Preserving Compilation for a 1+1D Lattice Shadow of φ⁴

A compilation architecture that constrains circuit synthesis using the symmetries of a periodic transverse-field Ising chain. The paper describes admitted gate schedules, a schedule-bound certificate, Q# emission, and a Lean operator-commutation development.

Scope: a lattice model and compiler design. The paper does not claim a continuum φ⁴ scattering amplitude.
